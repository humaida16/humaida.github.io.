FEATURED PROJECTS

Selected Work

01 — PROCUREMENT

Procurement Performance Dashboard

With ₦26.9B in procurement spend across 650 purchase orders, the challenge was to turn procurement activity into clear, actionable visibility. This dashboard brings spend, savings, supplier and delivery performance into one view, highlighting ₦1.8B in savings, a 40.5% on-time delivery rate and an average supplier rating of 3.54. It enables faster identification of spend patterns, performance gaps and areas requiring management attention.

02 — DATA ANALYTICS

Sales Performance Analysis

The business generated $55.8M in revenue and $32.7M in profit from 198K units sold across 12K customers. This dashboard was built to move beyond headline figures and uncover what was driving performance. It analyses revenue, profit, product categories, customer segments and geographic performance, revealing where sales were strongest and where opportunities for growth and improved performance existed.

03 — PUBLIC HEALTH

Nigeria Public Health Surveillance Dashboard

Public health surveillance can involve large volumes of information across locations, diseases and reporting facilities. This dashboard brings together 5M total population, 7K reported cases, 18K recorded deaths and 3K health events across 5 diseases and 5 reporting facilities. It makes patterns easier to explore by state, LGA, disease and year, helping transform complex surveillance data into a clearer picture of where health events and outcomes are concentrated.
