About Me

Procurement meets data.

I am a procurement professional with a growing focus on data analytics and business intelligence.

My work involves procurement operations, supplier management, purchase orders, vendor evaluation and using data to understand procurement performance.

I enjoy transforming raw information into clear dashboards, useful insights and practical recommendations that can support better decision-making.

My approach

I believe good data should do more than produce numbers. It should help people understand what is happening, why it is happening and what to do next.
