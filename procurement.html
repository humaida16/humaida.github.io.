Executive Summary

This project analyses procurement data to evaluate spend, savings, supplier performance and delivery efficiency.

The analysis covered ₦26.9B in procurement spend across 650 purchase orders, with ₦1.8B in reported savings. Only 40.5% of purchase orders were delivered on time, with 387 delayed POs identified.

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of procurement performance, helping identify high-spend areas, supplier risks and opportunities for operational improvement.

Business Problem

Procurement data contained valuable information across purchasing, supplier and delivery activities, but a consolidated view was needed to support better decision-making.

Objectives

  • Analyse procurement spend and savings.
  • Evaluate supplier delivery performance.
  • Identify delayed purchase orders and supplier risks.
  • Monitor procurement efficiency.
  • Develop an interactive management dashboard.

Data & Methodology

Data Preparation

Raw procurement data was cleaned and transformed using Power Query, including data-quality checks, standardisation and creation of analytical fields.

Analysis

DAX measures were developed to calculate procurement spend, savings, purchase order activity, on-time delivery, delayed POs, average delivery days and supplier performance.

Visualisation

An interactive Power BI dashboard was developed to provide an executive view of procurement activity and detailed supplier performance analysis.

Key Findings

  • Delivery performance requires attention: 40.5% of POs were delivered on time, while 387 were delayed.
  • Supplier performance varies: Delivery reliability and average delivery times differed across suppliers.
  • High-spend suppliers require closer monitoring: Suppliers combining significant procurement exposure with weak delivery performance represent greater operational risk.
  • Supplier ratings tell only part of the story: Combining ratings with actual delivery performance provides a more complete view of supplier effectiveness.
  • Spend concentration creates opportunities: High-spend categories provide opportunities for stronger cost management and strategic sourcing.

Recommendations

  • Strengthen supplier performance reviews for suppliers with recurring delivery issues.
  • Prioritise high-risk suppliers where high spend is combined with weak delivery performance.
  • Introduce corrective action plans for persistent delivery delays.
  • Combine delivery performance, supplier ratings, spend and delay history when evaluating suppliers.
