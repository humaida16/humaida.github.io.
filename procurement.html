Executive Summary

This project analyses procurement transactions to evaluate purchasing activity, expenditure, savings, supplier performance and delivery efficiency.

The analysis covered ₦26.9B in procurement spend across 650 purchase orders, with ₦1.8B in reported savings. Only 40.5% of purchase orders were delivered on time, with 387 delayed POs identified.

The analysis provides a consolidated view of procurement performance and highlights areas requiring closer supplier monitoring and operational improvement.

Recommendations

  • Strengthen supplier performance reviews for suppliers with recurring delivery issues.
  • Prioritise high-risk suppliers where significant procurement spend is combined with weak delivery performance.
  • Introduce corrective action plans for persistent delivery delays.
  • Combine delivery performance, supplier ratings, spend and delay history when evaluating suppliers.
  • Use high-spend categories to identify opportunities for stronger cost management and strategic sourcing.
